METRIC DEFINITIONS
RAP— Relative Advantage Percentage — your share of the time either player held a clear advantage.
"Advantage held" = one player leads in ships AND has equal-or-better production simultaneously. RAP only counts the time someone held that — so RAP(you) + RAP(opponent) always adds to 100%. It answers: of all the contested time, how much of it was yours?
Formula: RAP = (time you held advantage) / (time either player held advantage) × 100%
AAP— Absolute Advantage Percentage — your share of the entire match spent with a clear advantage.
Same "advantage held" definition as RAP, but the denominator is the full match length. AAP(you) + AAP(opponent) can add to less than 100% — the remainder was even or split-lead (timer active).
Formula: AAP = (time you held advantage) / (total match time) × 100%
Advantage— the 3-state machine underlying RAP and AAP.
Evaluated continuously throughout the match:
• Advantage held — one player leads ships AND has equal-or-better production.
• Timer active (split lead) — one player leads ships, the other leads production. Always scored as neutral. This is what the VS Timer measures.
• Even — ships and production are both tied.
FP— Float Pressure — how much of your fleet presence was on the enemy's side of the map, averaged across the whole match.
A time-integral of ship presence (fleets in transit + ships on planets) on the far side of the line of symmetry, divided by match duration. Measures sustained pressure regardless of whether it converted into captures.
Formula: FP = ∫(your ships on enemy-side) dt / match duration
📌 Example: if you averaged 30 ships worth of presence on the opponent's side throughout a 5-minute game, your FP is 30. If your opponent only averaged 10 ships on your side, their FP is 10.
FE— Float Effectiveness — how efficiently the ships you sent across the line converted into production.
Measures conversion, not presence. The numerator counts production income earned from enemy-side planets while you held them — not the ships you grabbed on capture. The denominator counts all ships sent toward enemy-side targets, win or lose.
Formula (float ledger): FE = Σ(prod/50 × seconds held, for planets you captured on the enemy side) / Σ(ships you landed on enemy-side planets you did not own, win or lose). Landings come from each fleet's ship-count drops on its current target (redirects respected); landings on one planet are grouped into an assault. Production is counted directly — the capture garrison and reinforcements are not counted. Every number (per assault, per fleet, whole game, all games) is a ratio of sums.
📌 Example: you land 50 ships on your opponent's home planet and hold it long enough to earn 25 ships of production before it's recaptured — that fleet's FE is 25/50 = 0.50. If you sent another fleet of 20 ships that got destroyed before capturing anything, FE = 25/70 ≈ 0.36.
VS Timer— seconds until the ship gap closes, signed by whoever's projected to even up first.
Leader = whoever currently leads production (tiebreak: ship lead if tied). Magnitude = how many seconds until the ship gap closes to zero, at the current production differential. Positive = P1 projected to even up, Negative = P2.
The chart has no artificial ceiling — when a player already dominates both metrics or production is exactly tied (no finite catch-up time exists), the line gaps rather than showing a fake value. Each side of the zero-line is scaled independently to that game's actual extremes.
Formula: VT = −(your ships − their ships) / ((your production − their production) / 50)
